## C16 — Companion benefit tracks capacity-to-act, not matched felt safety, with the observer channel controlled

**Derived from:** companion = Δg (coupling, access-route) vs companion = safety signal
(assumption-route); plus the second-reader channel of the absorption map §3.2, which
makes the earlier two-factor version of this test confounded and supersedes it.

**Prediction.** With felt-safety ratings matched AND the social/observer channel held
constant (same person, same social exposure), a companion who can actually intervene
extends tolerated engagement (or reduces avoidance) more than the same companion made
causally unable to intervene (distance, video-only, physically prevented). The
capacity effect must survive covarying felt safety.

**Consistent if:** capacity-manipulation studies (or natural contrasts: present vs
remote companion, adult vs trusted-but-helpless child companion) show behavioral
benefit tracking intervention capacity beyond reported reassurance.

**Inconsistent if (locked falsifier):** with felt safety matched and observer channel
controlled, capacity adds nothing — behavior tracks felt safety alone. Then the
companion is a signal, Δg is dead as a reading of the companion, and the dRRH
account of agoraphobia loses its central mechanism.

**Named rivals:** safety-signal (felt safety mediates); M_Cognitive (appraisal of
danger mediates; predicts believed reassurance transfers); perceived control.

**Sources to check:** companion-assisted / partner-assisted exposure trials in
agoraphobia; phone-contact and telepresence studies; safety-behaviour fading trials.

**Prior guess:** NO-DATA. I do not expect any existing study to have matched felt
safety while manipulating capacity; natural contrasts may allow a capped SUPPORT-N.

## C17 — Anticipatory dread carries the same cue-locked bias as in-situ measurement

**Derived from:** offline-replay reading of anticipatory dread (absorption map §3.1):
dread = running the horizon channel on a stored record of the situation.

**Prediction.** A person's anticipatory (imagery/planning-phase) horizon estimate for
a specific situation should correlate strongly with, and be predicted by, the same
person's in-situ estimate bias for that situation — one bias, two read modes — and
anticipatory probe slopes under imagery should behave like in-situ slopes.

**Consistent if:** imagery-phase and in-situ measures of situation-specific
threat/duration estimation correlate at the person-and-cue level.

**Inconsistent if (locked falsifier):** anticipatory and in-situ biases are
uncorrelated or dissociate by cue — then anticipatory dread is an independent channel,
the offline-replay reading fails, and modeling dread requires a new degree of freedom
that must be declared and Fisher-rank-counted before use, not absorbed silently.

**Named rivals:** M_Cognitive (worry/anticipatory processing as its own process —
predicts dissociation is possible and treatable separately); generic trait anxiety
(predicts correlation without cue specificity — cue-level specificity is what
separates).

**Sources to check:** imagery/behavioral-approach-test correlation studies in phobia
and agoraphobia; anticipatory anxiety vs in-situ fear literature.

**Prior guess:** NO-DATA on the probe-slope form; possible SUPPORT-N on coarse
correlation (shared with trait-anxiety rival).

## C19 — Governability changes before phenomenal fear in a definable subset

**Derived from:** the RGIF unit-of-treatment claim (v15 §rgif; S11): the therapeutic unit is
the completed information-bearing continuation under active phobic readout, so behavioral
governability (completion count, approach) should improve before fear ratings move in at
least a definable patient subset.

**Prediction.** In treatment courses (exposure-based or otherwise), sessions exist where
completion/approach metrics rise while concurrent fear ratings are unchanged; fear decline
lags. **Consistent if:** desynchrony literature documents behavior-before-fear ordering in
a nontrivial fraction of successful courses. **Inconsistent if (locked):** approach is only
ever observed after fear has already declined; fear-first ordering is universal.

**Named rivals:** cognitive model (appraisal change mediates and should precede or
accompany approach); habituation accounts (within-session fear decline drives approach).
**Note held before searching:** the fear-behavior "desynchrony" literature (Rachman &
Hodgson 1974 lineage) is known to exist by name only — NOT yet searched or read; this case
is registered before that search. **Prior guess:** SUPPORT-N (desynchrony is likely
documented, but rivals may accommodate it post hoc; discriminating power will need the
completion-count unit, not desynchrony alone).

## C20 — The interruptibility window lengthens with recovery

**Derived from:** RGIF operation 6 (meta-readout + alternative-policy access): recovery is
hypothesized to lengthen or open the latency from first collapse readout (prodrome/warning)
to committed episode (escape, faint, functional seizure), rather than to silence warnings.

**Prediction.** In conditions with measurable prodromes (vasovagal, functional seizures
under their own guideline pathway, phobic escape), clinical improvement correlates with a
longer or more often interrupted first-readout-to-commitment interval, at unchanged warning
frequency. **Inconsistent if (locked):** improvement tracks fewer warnings with an
unchanged window, or the window shows no relationship to course.

**Named rivals:** symptom-reduction accounts (improvement = fewer/weaker warnings).
**Prior guess:** NO-DATA — the window is unlikely to have been measured as such anywhere.

## C21 — Layer-specific intervention durability (P-EP1)

**Derived from:** REPL v0.1 Law 5 (sedimentation) + the recalibration law: if the founding
defect is a retention/typing error, phenomenal-fear-targeted intervention alone should be
less durable than record-retyping interventions; if the defect is a genuine physiological
state (short causal horizon), retyping alone must fail and causal repair must precede.
**Locked falsifier:** durability of outcome shows no interaction with error locus —
fear-targeted and ledger-targeted interventions are interchangeable across lesion types.
**Named rivals:** common-factors accounts (all credible interventions equivalent);
cognitive model (appraisal change is the common pathway). **Prior guess:** NO-DATA —
no existing trial classifies patients by error locus before assignment.

## C22 — Self-created evidence reversal (P-EP4)

**Derived from:** REPL Law 4 (readout-to-reality conversion): where feedback through
policy genuinely degrades the state (acrophobic stiffening), changing policy first must
measurably improve the physiological state afterwards (sway, recovery slopes), not only
reports. **Locked falsifier:** large, sustained policy change with no downstream
physiological-state improvement in a phenotype where the feedback loop is claimed —
Law 4 is then withdrawn for that phenotype. **Named rivals:** pure-appraisal accounts
(state never was degraded); pure-physiology accounts (state improvement drives policy,
not vice versa — distinguishable by temporal order). **Prior guess:** SUPPORT-N at best
(gait/posture literature may show post-treatment normalization without the temporal-order
analysis this case needs).

## C23 — Two-source open-data necessary-implication test (FROZEN before any data file was opened, 2026-09-02)

**Sources (unrelated paradigms, both world-class open repositories):**
- A: Zenodo 20796399 — VR spider exposure, subjective fear vs physiological stress (phobia/VR domain)
- B: Zenodo 321641 — PsPM-HRA1, delay fear conditioning SCR, 20 subjects (Pavlovian conditioning domain)
Only record METADATA and READMEs were inspected before this entry; no outcome files opened.

**Test A (Law-2 lane dissociation, necessary implication):** subjective-fear lane and
physiological lane must dissociate: (1) group-level |r| between mean subjective fear and
mean physiological reactivity ≤ 0.70; (2) both discordant quadrants (high-subj/low-phys
and low-subj/high-phys by median split) populated by ≥15% of participants.
**Locked falsifier:** |r| ≥ 0.85 (the K3 TOST bound) → lanes are one scalar in this
setting; the C-matrix adds nothing there. **Declared weakness:** dissociation is also
produced by measurement error alone → PASS = compatibility only, never validation.

**Test B (temporal-horizon readout, necessary implication):** in delay conditioning with
a fixed CS-US interval, anticipatory SCR must show temporal structure: (1) CS+ > CS−
anticipatory amplitude (discriminant sanity); (2) response-onset/peak latencies
concentrated within the CS-US window rather than uniform.
**Locked falsifier:** no CS+/CS− discrimination or temporally unstructured anticipatory
activity → no evidence of a remaining-time readout in this paradigm.
**Declared weakness:** both patterns are classic findings; PASS earns ordinal
compatibility from an unrelated paradigm, nothing more.

**Prior guesses:** A: PASS with r ≈ 0.3–0.6; B: PASS (classic). An unexpected FAIL on
either would be the more informative outcome and will be reported identically.

**SCORED 2026-09-02 (see S13):** A: PASS/PASS (r=0.119 cortisol, r=0.005 HR — far below
guess; quadrants 26–32%). B: amplitude MARGINAL (t=1.51, 12/20; frozen pipeline includes
habituation trials — limitation declared, no post-hoc rerun), timing PASS striking
(differential peak median 3.48s vs 3.5s deadline; 13/20 within 0.06s). Prior guess wrong
in the informative direction on A (dissociation stronger than guessed).

## C24 — Recovery order is predictable from lesion layer (FROZEN before any recovery-order literature search, 2026-09-02)

**Derived from:** the four severe-case probes (v19 §probes) + REPL layer taxonomy: if
layer diagnoses are real, the ORDER in which readout lanes recover under a working
intervention is predictable in advance from the layer of the lesion —
(a) upstream substrate repair (e.g. immunotherapy in anti-NMDAR) restores lanes in
dependency order; (b) policy-unlock interventions (lorazepam in catatonia, responsive
DBS in OCD) restore action/governability BEFORE phenomenal content shifts;
(c) cross-lane mismatch repair, if ever achieved (Capgras), restores felt familiarity
before belief revision.
**Locked falsifier:** recovery order across syndromes proves idiosyncratic and
unpredictable from layer — the taxonomy is then descriptive decoration and must be
called that. **Named rivals:** severity-based accounts (worst symptom recovers last
regardless of layer); common-pathway accounts (single recovery sequence for all).
**Prior guess:** NO-DATA for the full matrix; possible SUPPORT-N fragments in
catatonia (motor unlock before subjective relief) and DBS-OCD qualitative reports.
**Sources to check (unread):** longitudinal symptom-course studies in anti-NMDAR;
lorazepam response-order case series; DBS-OCD longitudinal phenomenology.

**Order commitment (repeating v1's lesson):** these two cases are registered BEFORE
reading the full texts named above. Any AI or human continuing this work reads
Rachman 1984 / Clark 1986 / Salkovskis 1991 in full only AFTER this file is committed
to the working directory, and scores C16–C17 under v1's four labels with the prior
guesses above reported win-or-lose.

### C25 — lane typing via MTMM inequality (Zenodo 5648055, IU-Hamburg fear conditioning, n≈66)
Holdings: record metadata + readme_iu_hamburg_reading.txt ONLY. No data CSV opened at freeze.
Lanes: RAT (subjective rating), SCR (electrodermal), FPS (startle). Per subject, per phase,
per lane: discrimination D = mean(CS+) − mean(CS−) over that phase's trials.
- C25.1 (PRIMARY, MTMM inequality): mean within-lane between-phase Spearman rank correlation of
  D across subjects (same lane, ACQ vs EX) **>** mean between-lane within-phase Spearman
  (different lanes, same phase; average over the 3 lane pairs × ACQ and EX).
  Rationale (frozen): under M0, between-lane/same-phase shares the same state and should exceed
  within-lane/cross-phase (state changed); under M1, lane-specific traits persist so
  within-lane/cross-phase exceeds between-lane. Direction is diagnostic BOTH ways.
  PASS = inequality holds as stated. FAIL = it reverses or ties (|difference| < 0.01 = FAIL).
- C25.2 (SECONDARY, end-of-extinction lane discordance, mirrors C23-A numbers): using each
  subject's D over the LAST 4 extinction trials for RAT and SCR: |r|(RAT_D, SCR_D) ≤ 0.70 AND
  both discordant quadrants by median split ≥ 15%. PASS = both. FAIL otherwise.
Exclusion rule (frozen): include only subjects present with non-missing D in all lanes/phases
entering a given criterion; report n per criterion. No other exclusions.
Prior guesses (recorded): C25.1 PASS p=0.7; C25.2 PASS p=0.6.
Falsifier for M1: C25.1 reversal (M0 pattern) is scored as a HIT AGAINST typed lanes.
