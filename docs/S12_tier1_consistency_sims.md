## A. Soeter & Kindt 2015 (Biol Psychiatry, PMID 25980916, abstract)
Target: abrupt avoidance→approach after reactivation+propranolol; reported fear
unchanged initially, declining months later.
- control: no approach, fear stays 10/10
- **T1 retype-only: NO approach** (p=1/9 → margin<0) — quantifies the Reconsolidation
  Bypass Problem: ledger retyping alone cannot produce the abrupt switch at this prior.
- **'amnesic' variant** (destabilization DELETES harm pseudo-counts, b:8→1 — an injected,
  declared assumption that is the literal semantics of an amnesic agent):
  first approach at encounter 0; fear 9.8 → 9.4 → ... → 1.7 over 24 encounters.
  → ORDINAL MATCH (behavior-first, fear-later), postdictive.
  → Refinement for the standing attack: RRHM reproduces the pattern IFF destabilization
    is modeled as prior-weight deletion — not derivable from the ledger, but coincides
    with the intervention's own claimed mechanism.

## B. Odgers et al. 2022 (Behav Res Ther SR/MA, PMID 36323055, metadata fully verified)
Target: single-session ≈ multi-session efficacy.
- p̂(1 session × 4 completions) = p̂(4 sessions × 1 completion) = 0.385
- p̂(many minutes, 0 completions) = 0.111
→ ORDINAL MATCH: outcome tracks completion count, not sessions/minutes — plus the
  discriminating prediction the SR cannot test: endured-minutes-without-completion ≈ 0.

## C. Re-encounter dependency (Mol Psychiatry 2025, PMID 39789312, abstract)
Target: lasting reduction ONLY when phobic cue re-encountered within days (not 4 weeks).
- window model: re-encounter@2d → durable change TRUE; @28d → FALSE.
→ STRUCTURAL MATCH ONLY — the 7-day window was chosen to reproduce the finding;
  this row is ILLUSTRATIVE and carries NO evidential weight (declared).
